Privacy Policy

U-Modbus · Effective November 19, 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy Policy

Summary

U-Modbus does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does with data

  • Device communication: U-Modbus talks directly from your Mac to the Modbus devices you configure, over your local network or a serial adapter. This traffic never passes through our servers.
  • Local storage: your devices, points and settings are saved only on your Mac (in the app’s sandbox container). Exported files are written only where you choose.
  • Network scanning: device discovery connects only to the addresses and ports you enter, and only when you start a scan.
  • Built-in demo devices: simulated devices listen only on your Mac’s loopback address (127.0.0.1) and are not reachable from other computers.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

U-Modbus is a technical tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: github.com/cyberumut/u-modbus/issues.
